# Turtle Practice Brand Style Guide

## Brand Philosophy
**"Steady & Grounded"** - Professional, calming, and purposeful design that embodies the turtle philosophy: slow and steady wins the race. Every element has purpose, nothing is frantic or busy.

## Typography

### Font Families

#### Headings (Serif - Wisdom & Timelessness)
- **Primary**: Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif
- **Web Alternative**: Merriweather (Google Fonts)
- **Weights**: Bold (700), Regular (400)
- **Characteristics**: Substantial, classic, conveys authority and wisdom

#### Body Text (Sans-Serif - Modern & Readable)
- **Primary**: 'Segoe UI', Tahoma, Geneva, Verdana, sans-serif
- **Web Alternative**: 'Open Sans', 'Lato' (Google Fonts)
- **Weights**: Regular (400), Semi-Bold (600)
- **Characteristics**: Clean, highly readable, professional

#### Quotes & Emphasis (Italic Serif)
- **Primary**: Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif (italic)
- **Use**: Pull quotes, testimonials, reflective statements

#### Monospace (Optional - for code/data)
- **Primary**: 'Courier New', Courier, monospace
- **Web Alternative**: 'Source Code Pro' (Google Fonts)

### Typography Scale

#### Presentations (Reveal.js)
- **H1 (Title Slide)**: 3.5em (56px) - Bold, Turtle Green
- **H2 (Section Headers)**: 2.5em (40px) - Bold, Charcoal
- **H3 (Sub-headers)**: 1.8em (29px) - Bold, Slate Gray
- **Body Text**: 1.5em (24px) - Regular, Charcoal
- **Small Text**: 1em (16px) - Regular, Slate Gray
- **Quotes**: 2em (32px) - Italic, Slate Gray

#### Print/PDF
- **H1**: 24pt - Bold
- **H2**: 18pt - Bold
- **H3**: 14pt - Bold
- **Body**: 11pt - Regular
- **Small**: 9pt - Regular

#### Web/Digital
- **H1**: 2.5rem (40px)
- **H2**: 2rem (32px)
- **H3**: 1.5rem (24px)
- **Body**: 1rem (16px)
- **Small**: 0.875rem (14px)

### Typography Rules
- **Line Height**: 1.6 for body text, 1.2 for headings
- **Letter Spacing**: Normal for body, slight increase (0.02em) for all-caps
- **Paragraph Spacing**: 1.5em between paragraphs
- **Max Width**: 65-75 characters per line for optimal readability

## Design Principles

### Layout
1. **Generous White Space**: Let content breathe - embrace negative space
2. **Asymmetric Balance**: Left or right alignment over centered (more dynamic)
3. **Visual Hierarchy**: Clear distinction between primary, secondary, tertiary elements
4. **Grid-Based**: Use consistent spacing units (8px base unit)

### Imagery
1. **Style**: Natural, authentic photography - no overly staged corporate shots
2. **Subjects**: Ocean scenes, turtles, paths/journeys, sunrise/sunset, natural textures
3. **Treatment**: Subtle warm filter, slightly desaturated for calm feel
4. **Text Overlay**: Use dark overlay (40-60% opacity) for readability on images
5. **Size**: Large, impactful images - don't be timid

### Motion & Transitions
1. **Speed**: Slow and deliberate (800-1000ms) - never rushed
2. **Easing**: Ease-out curves - natural deceleration
3. **Type**: Fades over slides, subtle zooms, gentle reveals
4. **Philosophy**: Movement should feel intentional, not flashy

### Iconography
1. **Style**: Line icons or simple solids - not overly detailed
2. **Stroke Weight**: 2-3px for consistency
3. **Color**: Turtle Green or Charcoal
4. **Size**: Consistent scale, generous spacing

## Content Voice & Tone

### Voice Characteristics
- **Wise but Approachable**: Like a trusted mentor
- **Calm but Confident**: Steady, not tentative
- **Reflective but Actionable**: Balance philosophy with practical steps
- **Honest but Hopeful**: Acknowledge challenges, focus on solutions

### Writing Guidelines
- Use short, powerful sentences for impact
- Ask rhetorical questions to engage
- Balance data/evidence with story/emotion
- Active voice for instructions, passive for reflection
- Minimal jargon - accessible to all audiences

### Example Phrases (Brand Voice)
✅ "The tortoise had a plan for race day."
✅ "Who gets the best of you?"
✅ "Some is better than none. Now is better than later."
✅ "Don't allow the shame of delay to stall your start."

❌ "Optimize your productivity metrics for maximum ROI"
❌ "Leverage synergistic workflows"
❌ "Disrupt your daily paradigm"

## Presentation-Specific Guidelines

### Slide Structure
1. **One Idea Per Slide**: Don't crowd - let concepts land
2. **Text Minimum**: 3-7 words for impact slides, max 20-30 for content
3. **Visual Dominance**: Image takes 60-70% of space when used
4. **Consistent Margins**: 10% padding on all sides

### Special Slide Types

#### Title Slide
- Large hero image (turtle)
- Minimal text overlay: Title + Your name
- Text position: Lower third or left third
- Background: Dark overlay for contrast

#### Quote Slide
- Solid color background (Turtle Green or Soft Cream)
- Large quote text (2-2.5em)
- Attribution small and subtle
- Optional: Subtle texture or pattern

#### Data/List Slide
- Left-aligned text
- Generous line spacing (2em between items)
- Use bullets sparingly - numbers or icons better
- Highlight key item in Coral Orange

#### Transition Slide
- Single word or short phrase
- Centered
- Solid background color
- Signals shift to new topic

## File Naming Conventions

### Images
- Format: `category-description-variant.ext`
- Examples:
  - `turtle-hero-opening.jpg`
  - `background-ocean-light.jpg`
  - `icon-calendar-line.svg`
  - `photo-sunrise-beach-01.jpg`

### Assets
- Lowercase with hyphens
- Descriptive, not generic
- Include size variant if multiple: `-sm`, `-md`, `-lg`, `-xl`

## Usage Examples

### Good Design Decisions
✅ Large turtle image with dark overlay + white text
✅ Quote slide with turtle green background + cream text
✅ Clean slide with single question in large type
✅ Generous margins - content doesn't touch edges
✅ Consistent color use - turtle green for all headings

### Avoid
❌ Busy backgrounds that compete with text
❌ Too many colors - stick to palette
❌ Centered blocks of paragraph text
❌ Small, timid images in corners
❌ Rapid animations or transitions
❌ All-caps body text (headings okay)

## Accessibility Considerations

### Color Contrast
- All text meets WCAG AA standards (4.5:1 for body, 3:1 for large)
- Don't rely on color alone to convey meaning
- Test designs in grayscale

### Readability
- Minimum 24px for presentation body text
- Avoid light gray on white
- Use sufficient line height
- Break up long content sections
