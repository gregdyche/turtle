Your Turtle Journey Check-In

Consistency is the turtle's superpower. Regular check-ins keep you moving steadily toward your Eastward Gaze.

Use these tools to track your progress, reflect on your journey, and make course corrections when needed.

📅 Daily Check-In

Start each day with intention and end with reflection.

  • Morning: What's my deliberate first step?
  • Midday: Am I on track? Having fun?
  • Evening: What act of love did I practice?

📋 Weekly Review

Reflect on your weekly intentions and plan the next seven days.

  • Did I complete my weekly intentions?
  • What adjustments do I need to make?
  • How will I protect my A-Zone next week?

🗓️ Monthly Preview

  • What calendar blocking do I need?

🚨 Early Warning Signs

Watch for these signals that you might be drifting off course:

  • Skipping daily reflection for multiple days
  • A-Zone time getting squeezed out regularly
  • Feeling overwhelmed or constantly busy
  • Missing weekly or monthly reviews
  • Losing sight of your Eastward Gaze

Remember: The turtle course-corrects early and often. Small adjustments keep you moving in the right direction.

Remember: The turtle wins the race not through speed, but through persistence and direction. 🐢
