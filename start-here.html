Welcome to the Turtle Path

In the Air Force, we had a saying: "Look out the window." When an aircraft crashes, it's often because the crew was heads-down in checklists while the plane flew into a mountain. Everyone was doing their job professionally—but nobody was looking out the window.

You're busy. You're professional. You're trained. But when was the last time you looked out the window of your own life?

This is your moment to pause, look up from the checklist, and ask: "How did I get here? What have I done?"

The turtle path isn't about productivity tricks. It's about choosing to be alive instead of becoming the walking dead. It starts with a decision: "I do not have the right to be a lump on a log."

1

Look Out the Window

Stop the checklist grind. Step back and honestly assess where you are. Are you just operating your personal machine, or are you leading your life toward something meaningful?

Standing in that dark parking lot, I had a decision: become the walking dead or choose to be alive. You have the same choice. "I do not have the right to be a lump on a log."

3

Who Gets the Best of You?

The eye-opening question that changed everything. Who gets your A-game? Who gets your best energy during your peak hours? Strangers and clients, or the people you claim to love?

Audit Your Energy
4

Know Your Daily Zones

Your biology determines your best times. A-zone (7am-1pm): highest energy, analytical work. B-zone (2-8pm): creativity, meetings. Rest zone: recharge for tomorrow's A-zone.

Map Your Zones
5

Pay Yourself First

If you don't invest in yourself first, you won't make progress on anything that matters. Like the oxygen mask rule: you can't help others if you're depleted.

Schedule Self-Investment
6

Understand The Scorecard

There's a secret scorecard we can't escape: how we treat others. This is the universal measure we all grade ourselves on, whether we believe in God or not.

Reflect on Relationships
7

What You Want to Be Remembered For

Peter Drucker's question: What do you want to be remembered for? Who do you want to be remembered by? What do they find important? This sets your true direction.

Define Your Legacy
8

Set Your Eastward Gaze

Like turtle migration, you need a clear direction. What's the one thing that matters most this year? This becomes your North Star for all decisions.

Choose Your Direction
9

Create Your Non-Agenda

Success isn't just about what you do—it's about what you choose NOT to do. If you don't make room for good ideas, it doesn't matter how good they are.

Define Your Stops
10

Build Your Ordinary Tuesday

The extraordinary life comes from ordinary days done well. Design a sustainable routine that you can maintain—marathon pace, not sprint. It's the will to practice, not the will to win.

Design Your Day
11

Install Yellow Alarms

Don't wait for red alarms (breakdown, divorce, crisis). Use weekly reflection, the DICE model, and course corrections to adjust before it's painful. Call your shots.

Set Up Reviews

Remember: This is a Marathon, Not a Sprint

The turtle wins through persistence, not speed. Some is better than none. Now is better than later. Start where you are with what you have—even if it's ugly at first.

The best time to plant a tree was 20 years ago. The second best time is today.
